Cascading Multiplier: 3-5x

Supply chain disruptions create cascading effects throughout the economy. A one-week infrastructure disruption typically requires 4-6 weeks for full recovery. The multiplier effect means direct impacts are amplified through dependent industries and services.

Supply Chain Multiplier Effect

Typical Range 3x to 5x
Direct → Indirect Impact $1 → $3-5
Recovery Timeline 4-6 weeks per week of disruption

COVID-19 Supply Chain Precedent

Peak Trade Decline -20% to -30%
GDP Impact -30% (April 2020)
Recovery Duration 8+ months to previous levels
COVID Example: Trade decline 20-30% → GDP decline ~30% Implies multiplier of 1.0-1.5x for 2-3 month disruption Supply chain literature: 3-5x for targeted infrastructure (Studies: Journal of Supply Chain Management, PMC databases)

Methodology Summary

Data Integration Approach

All figures are derived from official government databases, regulatory filings, and peer-reviewed research. Where precise data is unavailable, conservative estimates use:

  • Historical precedent from similar disruptions
  • Industry-provided estimates (JPMorgan, AAR)
  • Supply chain multiplier literature (3-5x range)
  • Equity market composition (80% equities)

Key Assumptions

  • Trade value: 365-day average of 2024 actual trade flows
  • Port impacts: Based on historical strike data and terminal throughput
  • Rail impacts: 2022 AAR estimates for nationwide disruption
  • Refinery impacts: Daily output × product mix pricing
  • Border impacts: Annual crossing data ÷ 365
  • Multiplier: 3-5x based on supply chain literature
  • Market impacts: COVID-19 precedent (30% decline)
